Why test under load

A generator that's never carried real load is a generator you're guessing about.

Standby gensets can sit for months without a real workout. A load bank puts them under an artificial electrical load so you find out how they perform before an outage forces the question.

Commissioning

New installs need to prove out at rated capacity before they're signed off. We size the load to match the spec and document the run.

Wet-stacking prevention

Diesel gensets that idle at low load build up unburned fuel in the exhaust. Periodic load bank testing burns that off and protects the engine.

Compliance & readiness

Facilities under NFPA 110 or insurance-driven testing requirements need a documented load test on a set schedule. We handle the run.
